How to Pass Bureau of Immigration (BI) Inspection

Essential advice for smooth primary & secondary inspection at NAIA, MCIA, or CRK checkpoints.

1

Attire & Confidence

Wear neat, smart-casual attire. Answer questions directly without elaborating unnecessary stories unless asked.

2

Printed Document Folder

Keep return flight tickets, hotel vouchers, and COE in a dedicated clear folder ready for instant presentation.

3

Govt Workers & JO/COS

Always secure a formal **Travel Authority (TA)** or formal **Leave Clearance / Agency Head Letter** even for personal vacations.

4

Financial Verification

Be ready to show active mobile banking apps, bank certificates, or company ID proving your active income in the PH.

5

eTravel Registration

Complete official registration at etravel.gov.ph within 72 hours prior to scheduled departure time.

6

Sponsored Trips

If sponsored by 1st/2nd degree relative, bring PSA birth/marriage certificates. For non-relatives, formal notarized AOSG is mandatory.
